# Ramsay Maintenance Test Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for Ramsay Corporation's maintenance knowledge tests: **MecTest** and **MultiCrafTest**, plus their trainee-level versions, Mechanical Maintenance Trainee and MultiCraft Trainee. It covers how the tests work, a clear method for each of the nine content areas, 94 original practice items with full explanations and 24 diagrams, what Ramsay says about scoring, and study plans for a week or a weekend. Written for maintenance mechanics, millwrights and multicraft technicians. Facts about the tests come only from Ramsay Corporation's own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free Ramsay Maintenance practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance), or the shorter [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find out which form you will sit.** MecTest is mechanical only. MultiCrafTest adds electrical. The [test overview](docs/test-overview.md#which-form-you-might-get) explains the four forms.
2. **Find your weak areas.** Take the [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has three items from each content area and tells you where to start.
3. **Fix the weakest area first.** Open its guide below, learn the method, then do its practice items.
4. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your test date is close.

## Quick facts

| | MecTest | MultiCrafTest |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher | Ramsay Corporation | Ramsay Corporation |
| Questions | 60, multiple choice | 60, multiple choice |
| Level | Journey (2 to 5 years of training and experience) | Journey (2 to 5 years of training and experience) |
| Content | 8 mechanical categories | 7 mechanical and electrical categories |
| Trainee version | Mechanical Maintenance Trainee, 60 items | MultiCraft Trainee, 60 items |
| Time limit | Not published by Ramsay. Check your invitation | Not published by Ramsay. Check your invitation |
| Pass mark | None published. Each employer sets its own | None published. Each employer sets its own |
| Published average | 38.40 out of 60 | 29.01 out of 60 |
| Format | Paper or online, always proctored | Paper or online, always proctored |
| Languages | English, Spanish version available | English, Spanish version available |

Sources: Ramsay's [MecTest](https://www.ramsaycorp.com/product/?id=1186) and [MultiCrafTest](https://www.ramsaycorp.com/product/?id=1822) product pages and its [FAQ](https://www.ramsaycorp.com/resources/faq/), checked 24 November 2026.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Skip what is not on your form.** MecTest and Mechanical Maintenance Trainee candidates can skip the three electrical pages.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most say why the nearest wrong option is wrong, which is where the learning is.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write whether it was knowledge or method. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the principles. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want your own annotated copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items and diagrams

All practice items, explanations and diagrams are original, written and drawn for these notes. None are taken from Ramsay's tests or materials, or from any prep site. They follow the content categories Ramsay publishes so that what you learn here transfers to the real test.
