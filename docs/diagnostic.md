# 7-minute diagnostic

One or two original items from each content area, to show you where to start. Answer without looking anything up, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

- **Sitting a MultiCraft form?** Do all nine parts, 12 items, in 7 minutes.
- **Sitting MecTest or Mechanical Maintenance Trainee?** Do parts A to F only, 8 items, in 5 minutes. Parts G to I are electrical and are not on those forms.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from any Ramsay test.

If you already know your weak spots, skip this and take the [free Ramsay Maintenance practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of pacing.

## Part A: Hydraulics and pneumatics

**D1.** In a hydraulic jack, a 50 lb push acts on a small piston of 1 sq in. The large piston has an area of 20 sq in. Ignoring friction, what force can the large piston lift?

- A. 50 lb
- B. 70 lb
- C. 1,000 lb
- D. 2,500 lb

**D2.** What is the main job of the receiver tank in a compressed air system?

- A. To store air and smooth out pressure swings
- B. To add oil to the air
- C. To raise air temperature
- D. To filter out water vapour completely

## Part B: Welding and rigging

**D3.** Which welding process uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode?

- A. SMAW (stick)
- B. GMAW (MIG)
- C. GTAW (TIG)
- D. Flux-cored arc welding

**D4.** A 1,000 lb load hangs from two equal sling legs, each at 45° from horizontal. About how much tension is in each leg?

- A. 500 lb
- B. 707 lb
- C. 1,000 lb
- D. 1,414 lb

## Part C: Power transmission and lubrication

**D5.** A 15-tooth gear turning at 900 rpm drives a 45-tooth gear. How fast does the 45-tooth gear turn?

- A. 300 rpm
- B. 450 rpm
- C. 900 rpm
- D. 2,700 rpm

## Part D: Pumps, piping and combustion

**D6.** What does it mean to prime a centrifugal pump?

- A. To fill its casing and suction line with liquid before starting
- B. To paint it
- C. To run it dry for a minute
- D. To close the suction valve

## Part E: Print reading

**D7.** A length is dimensioned 1.250 +0.002 / -0.000. What is the largest acceptable length?

- A. 1.248
- B. 1.250
- C. 1.252
- D. 1.270

## Part F: Mechanical maintenance and shop tools

**D8.** What is a feeler gauge used for?

- A. Measuring small gaps
- B. Measuring shaft speed
- C. Checking thread pitch on bolts over 2 inches
- D. Measuring temperature

## Part G: Motors and controls (MultiCraft only)

**D9.** What does a motor overload relay protect against?

- A. Lightning strikes
- B. Sustained overcurrent that would overheat the motor
- C. Low oil level
- D. Short circuits only

## Part H: Electronics, PLCs and instruments (MultiCraft only)

**D10.** How is an ammeter connected in a circuit?

- A. In series
- B. In parallel
- C. Across the supply
- D. With power off

## Part I: AC/DC theory and power distribution (MultiCraft only)

**D11.** What current flows when 12 V is applied across a 4 Ω resistor?

- A. 0.33 A
- B. 3 A
- C. 8 A
- D. 48 A

**D12.** What is the combined resistance of two 10 Ω resistors in parallel?

- A. 5 Ω
- B. 10 Ω
- C. 20 Ω
- D. 100 Ω

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D12</summary>

**D1: C, 1,000 lb.** Pressure = 50 lb / 1 sq in = 50 psi. Force on the large piston = 50 x 20 = 1,000 lb.

**D2: A.** The receiver stores compressed air so the compressor does not cycle constantly, and evens out pressure swings. *Why not D:* some water settles in the receiver, but removing vapour completely is a dryer's job.

**D3: C.** GTAW (TIG) uses a tungsten electrode that is not consumed. *Why not B:* GMAW feeds a consumable wire.

**D4: B, about 707 lb.** Each leg carries 500 lb x 1.414 = 707 lb. *Why not A:* 500 lb ignores the sling angle.

**D5: A, 300 rpm.** 900 x 15 / 45 = 300 rpm. Small driving large slows down.

**D6: A.** A centrifugal pump cannot pump air, so its casing and suction must be full of liquid before it starts.

**D7: C, 1.252.** The upper limit is 1.250 + 0.002 = 1.252. The lower limit is 1.250.

**D8: A.** Feeler gauges are thin blades of known thickness used to measure gaps such as valve clearances and coupling gaps.

**D9: B.** Overload relays trip on sustained overcurrent. *Why not D:* fuses and breakers handle short circuits.

**D10: A.** Current must pass through the meter, so it goes in series.

**D11: B, 3 A.** I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.

**D12: A, 5 Ω.** Two equal resistors in parallel give half of one: 10 / 2 = 5 Ω.

</details>
