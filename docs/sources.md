## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice or general trade knowledge, not Ramsay statements:

- the per-item timing budgets and checkpoints
- the "45 or more out of 60 on practice" target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- the technical content of the section guides, which is standard maintenance, fluid power, welding, rigging, drawing and electrical practice explained in our own words
- all practice items, diagrams and explanations, which are original
