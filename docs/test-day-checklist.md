# Test-day checklist

Ramsay tests are always proctored, either on paper at a test site or online with a supervisor from the company or a third party. Run through this list before you start.

## The day before

- [ ] Confirm the test name, the time, the place or login link, and what ID to bring.
- [ ] Ask whether a calculator is allowed, and if so what kind. Do not assume.
- [ ] Confirm any accommodation you asked for, such as extra time.
- [ ] Reread the formulas: F = P x A, driver x size = driven x size, V = I x R, P = V x I, 120 x f / poles.
- [ ] Short warm-up only. No full practice test the night before.
- [ ] Sleep. Most lost marks are careless slips on easy items.

## One hour before

- [ ] Eat something and bring water if allowed.
- [ ] If testing online, use the device and connection the proctor approved, close other programs and silence your phone.
- [ ] If testing on paper, bring two sharp pencils and an eraser unless told they are provided.
- [ ] Use the bathroom.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the instructions fully. Note the time limit and whether you can go back to earlier items.
- [ ] Work out your checkpoints: about 15 items at a quarter of the time, 30 at half, 45 at three quarters.
- [ ] If scrap paper is allowed, write the formulas down before you start.

## During the test

- [ ] Hydraulics: name each component, then trace the flow. Subtract the rod area on retract.
- [ ] Gears and belts: sanity check the direction of change. Count meshes for direction.
- [ ] Rigging: split the load, then apply the angle factor.
- [ ] Prints: write the dimension chain. Work out both tolerance limits.
- [ ] Controls and PLCs: contacts are drawn at rest. Separate the instruction from the wiring.
- [ ] Circuits: series adds resistance, parallel shares voltage.
- [ ] Stuck for more than twice your average time? Pick your best answer and move on.
- [ ] Never leave a blank.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down which categories felt weak while you still remember. If you retest later, start there.
- [ ] Ask the employer how and when you will hear the result, and what the retest policy is.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day or two, do one full timed run on the [free Ramsay Maintenance practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance) and review only the items you got wrong.
