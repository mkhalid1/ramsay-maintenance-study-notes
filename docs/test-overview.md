# The Ramsay maintenance tests explained

Ramsay Corporation is a US test publisher, founded in 1973, that specialises in assessing skilled trades employees. When people say "the Ramsay maintenance test", they usually mean one of two journey-level forms: **MecTest** for mechanical maintenance jobs, or **MultiCrafTest** for multicraft jobs that need both mechanical and electrical skills. Ramsay also publishes trainee-level versions of both. This page sets out what each form covers, based on Ramsay's own product pages.

## Which form you might get

Ramsay groups its tests by job level. The four forms these notes cover are:

| Form | Level | Items | Who it is for, in Ramsay's words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanical Maintenance Trainee, Form A (RR31-A / OL31-A) | Trainee | 60 | Selecting mechanical maintenance trainees with one year of training or experience |
| MecTest, Form A1 (RR16-A / OL16-A) | Journey | 60 | Measuring journey-level mechanical knowledge and skills for maintenance jobs, most often repair or maintenance mechanics |
| MultiCraft Trainee, Form A3 (RR116-A / OL116-A) | Trainee | 60 | Applicants or employees with at least one year of mechanical and electrical training or experience |
| MultiCrafTest, Form A6 (RR100-A / OL100-A) | Journey | 60 | Selecting maintenance employees with knowledge in seven craft areas |

Ramsay defines **trainee** as 2 to 3 years of training or experience, needing another 1 to 2 years, and **journey** as 2 to 5 years of training and experience. It also sells higher-level and alternative forms, such as the Maintenance Troubleshooter (an alternative to MultiCrafTest without board-level electronics), the Millwright test and the C, B and A Mechanic Evaluations. If your invitation names one of those, the content areas overlap with these notes but are not identical.

Not sure which one you will sit? Ask the recruiter or HR contact for the test name. It is a normal question and it changes what you should study.

Ramsay's exact category names are:

- **MecTest:** Hydraulics & Pneumatics; Print Reading; Welding & Rigging; Power Transmission; Lubrication; Pumps & Piping; Mechanical Maintenance; Shop Machines, Tools & Equipment.
- **Mechanical Maintenance Trainee:** Hydraulics & Pneumatics; Print Reading; Welding & Rigging; Power Transmission & Lubrication; Pumps & Piping; Maintenance; Shop Machines & Tools, Materials & Equipment.
- **MultiCrafTest:** Hydraulics and Pneumatics; Welding and Rigging; Power Transmission, Lubrication, Mechanical Maintenance, and Shop Machines, Tools & Equipment; Pumps, Piping, and Combustion; Motors, Control Circuits, and Schematics & Print Reading; Digital Electronics, Power Supplies, Computers & PLC, and Test Instruments; Basic AC/DC Theory, Power Distribution, and Electrical Maintenance.

Ramsay does not publish how many of the 60 items fall in each category, so these notes do not guess.

## How the test is given

- **Paper or online.** Each form has a paper version (SKU starting RR) and an online version (SKU starting OL). Ramsay says form names change when tests are updated, but the SKU stays the same.
- **Always proctored.** Ramsay's FAQ says all its tests, paper or online, must be proctored. Online tests must be supervised by a company representative or a third-party agent.
- **Scrambled versions.** Ramsay offers scrambled online versions of many tests, where question order is randomised within each category, for security when people test close together or retest.
- **Accommodations.** Ramsay's FAQ lists extra time, having the test read aloud, or a quiet room as examples. Ask the employer when you are scheduled.

## What the questions look like

Every form is multiple choice with four options. Ramsay publishes one sample item per form. MecTest's sample asks how peak hydraulic demand can be met. MultiCraft Trainee's asks what electrical resistance is measured in. MultiCrafTest's asks about a stage inside a microprocessor. Mechanical Maintenance Trainee's asks you to name a labelled part on a cylinder diagram. So expect plain knowledge questions alongside diagram questions.
