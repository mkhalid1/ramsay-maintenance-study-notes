# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the test date is already set. Both assume you start with the [7-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weak areas. Expect about an hour a day on the 7-day plan.

MecTest and Mechanical Maintenance Trainee candidates can skip the electrical days and spend the time on their weakest mechanical areas instead.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md) and find out which form you will sit. Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your three weakest parts. |
| 2 | Fluid power and pumps | Work through [hydraulics and pneumatics](sections/hydraulics-pneumatics.md) and [pumps and piping](sections/pumps-piping.md). Draw the five fluid power symbols from memory at the end. |
| 3 | Drives and rigging | Work through [power transmission](sections/power-transmission-lubrication.md) and [welding and rigging](sections/welding-rigging.md). Learn the sling angle table. |
| 4 | Prints and tools | Work through [print reading](sections/print-reading.md) and [maintenance and shop tools](sections/maintenance-shop-tools.md). Practise three micrometer readings. |
| 5 | Electrical (MultiCraft) | Work through [motors and controls](sections/motors-controls.md), [electronics and PLCs](sections/electronics-plc-instruments.md) and [AC/DC theory](sections/ac-dc-power-distribution.md). MecTest candidates: redo your two weakest mechanical sections instead. |
| 6 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=5q2ftk&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance). Score it by category. Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 7 | Fix and rest | Redo the items you missed this week without looking at the answers. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Was it knowledge or method?** Did you not know the fact, or did you know it and slip (wrong ratio direction, forgot to subtract the rod area)?
2. **What in the question should have stopped me?** Name the word, number or symbol.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "sanity check: small driving big slows down".

Keep these in one list. By day 7 the same few slips will keep showing up, and fixing them is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test is close.

### Day 1: find and fix the gaps

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 30 min | Read "A method that works" and "Traps that cost points" on every section page for your form. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 45 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest areas. Read every explanation. |
| 4 | 45 to 60 min | Take a timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=5q2ftk&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance). |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Redo yesterday's misses without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Reread the formulas: F = P x A, driver x size = driven x size, V = I x R, P = V x I, 120 x f / poles. |
| 3 | 10 min | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 4 | Test | Arrive or log in early, with whatever ID the invitation asks for. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then sleep.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** Ramsay's tests are copyrighted and it sells alternate and scrambled forms, so recalled questions will not match yours. Learn the principles instead.
- **Do not study only your trade.** A mechanic sitting MultiCrafTest still needs the electrical categories, and an electrician still needs the mechanical ones.
- **Do not cram the night before.** Tired candidates slip on easy sums, which is where most of the marks are.
