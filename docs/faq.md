# Frequently asked questions

These are the questions people search for most about the Ramsay tests, taken from Google's "People also ask" results and the related questions Google showed for US searches on "ramsay test", "ramsay maintenance test", "ramsay mechanical test" and "mectest" in November 2026. Answers stick to what Ramsay Corporation states, and say so when an answer is our guidance.

## What is the Ramsay test?

A family of employment tests published by Ramsay Corporation, which has been in business since 1973 and focuses on skilled trades. For maintenance jobs, the best known are MecTest (mechanical maintenance) and MultiCrafTest (mechanical and electrical maintenance), each with 60 multiple-choice items. You will also see it spelled "Ramsey", but the publisher is Ramsay. See the [test overview](test-overview.md).

## What are the different types of Ramsay tests?

Ramsay groups its tests into electrical and controls/instrumentation, mechanical, multicraft, diagnostic, maintenance planning and facilities maintenance, other crafts, aptitude, basic skills and people skills. Within each group there are tests for apprentice/entry, trainee, journey and above-journey levels. These notes cover the trainee and journey mechanical and multicraft knowledge tests.

## What is the Ramsay test used for?

Ramsay says its tests are used for pre-employment hiring, promotion, pay-for-knowledge and training diagnostics. See [for maintenance candidates](maintenance-candidates.md).

## How many questions are on the Ramsay test?

MecTest, MultiCrafTest, MultiCraft Trainee and Mechanical Maintenance Trainee each have 60 multiple-choice items. Other Ramsay tests vary. The Mechanical Aptitude Test has 36 items, for example, and the Multi-Skilled Maintenance Mechanic test has 100.

## How long is the Ramsay maintenance test?

Ramsay's product pages do not publish a time limit for these forms. Your invitation or the first screen of the test will tell you. The [timing guide](timing.md) shows how to budget once you know.

## What are the key topics covered in the Ramsay test?

For MecTest: hydraulics and pneumatics, print reading, welding and rigging, power transmission, lubrication, pumps and piping, mechanical maintenance, and shop machines, tools and equipment. MultiCrafTest adds motors and control circuits, electronics and PLCs, and AC/DC theory and power distribution. The [test overview](test-overview.md#what-each-form-covers) maps every category to a section guide.

## What is a maintenance aptitude test?

Strictly, an aptitude test measures your ability to learn, using everyday mechanical or electrical ideas, and Ramsay uses aptitude tests for apprentice and entry roles. In job ads, though, "maintenance aptitude test" is often used loosely for knowledge tests such as MecTest, which measure what you already know about maintenance work. Ask the employer which kind you will sit.

## What are the names of some common mechanical aptitude tests?

Well-known ones include the Ramsay Mechanical Aptitude Test (MAT), the Bennett Mechanical Comprehension Test (BMCT) from Pearson, and the Wiesen Test of Mechanical Aptitude (WTMA). These test reasoning about everyday mechanical principles. MecTest and MultiCrafTest are different: they test trade knowledge.

## How do I prepare for the Ramsay test?

Find out which form you will sit, take the [7-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md), study your weakest areas with the section guides, then do a timed run. The [study plans](study-plans.md) lay this out for a week or for two days. Ramsay itself says it does not sell preparation material and suggests textbooks from a library, community college or bookstore for the subject areas.

## Where can I find a free Ramsay practice test with questions and answers?

Ramsay does not publish practice tests, and says it has not sanctioned any online practice materials. This repo has original practice items with answers and explanations in every [section guide](test-overview.md#what-each-form-covers), and PrepClubs offers a [free Ramsay Maintenance practice test](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ramsay-maintenance?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ramsay-maintenance) in the MecTest and MultiCraft style. Neither is official Ramsay material.

## Can I find MecTest answers online?

Real MecTest questions are copyrighted, and Ramsay's FAQ says they may not be copied or distributed. Ramsay also sells alternate and scrambled forms, so any "answers" you find are unlikely to match your test. Learning the principles is the reliable route.

## What are some sample questions for the Walmart maintenance assessment?

We cannot confirm which test any particular employer uses, so ask the recruiter for the test name. If it turns out to be a Ramsay form, Ramsay publishes one sample item per test on its product pages, and the section guides here have original items in the same style for every MecTest and MultiCraft category.

## Is it hard to pass a mechanical aptitude test?

It depends on the cut score the employer sets, which Ramsay does not publish. Ramsay's own figures show average scores of about 38 out of 60 on MecTest and about 29 out of 60 on MultiCrafTest, so most people miss a fair number of items. See [how scoring works](scoring.md).

## Is there a pass mark?

No universal one. Ramsay's FAQ says it cannot recommend a cut score without a job analysis and validation study for the employer, and that cut scores vary with the job and the labour market.

## Can I retake the test?

Ramsay's FAQ says employers should give candidates the opportunity to retest under a written policy, ideally with an alternate form. The waiting period is set by the employer.

## Is the test available in Spanish?

Yes for these four forms. Ramsay's product pages say MecTest, MultiCrafTest, MultiCraft Trainee and Mechanical Maintenance Trainee are also available in Spanish.

## Is the test proctored?

Yes. Ramsay's FAQ says all its tests, paper or online, must be proctored.
